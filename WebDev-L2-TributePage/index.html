Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India • People's President • Visionary

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions of students through his life and work.

About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. From a young age, he developed a strong interest in science and technology.

He worked with India's space and defence programmes and played an important role in the development of India's missile technology.

In 2002, Dr. Kalam became the 11th President of India. He was especially admired by students and young people because of his vision for India's future.

Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture to students. His life and teachings continue to inspire people across the world.
